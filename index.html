A Day at the Farm

Hover over a word to hear it and see its Russian translation.
On a phone, tap a word.

It is a fine day in May. Jake and his sister Kate go to a farm.

They see a big brown cow next to a house. The cow is loud! It says, “Moo! Moo!”

A gray mouse is in the house. It has a pink nose and a long tail. Now the mouse is under a black chair.

“Look! A mouse!” say Kate and Jake.

Jake sees a chick next to a box. The chick is not big. It is yellow.

Now the sky is gray. They can see a big cloud.

“Oh no! Rain!” say Jake and Kate.

Jake and Kate rush back to the house. They sit on a soft red mat and have a snack.

“What a good day!” say Kate and Jake.
